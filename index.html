About The Sun ☀️

Dr. Nikkia Gumbs, known publicly as The Sun, is a human systems thinker, speaker, writer, and workplace educator. Her work explores grief, culture, nervous system literacy, burnout, loneliness, AI, and the design of human-centered systems.

She brings research-grounded ideas into rooms that need more honesty, more humanity, and better frameworks for how people actually live and work.
